What's new in 2.1

• Photo details — tap the ⓘ in the photo viewer to see filename, dimensions, file size, and camera info (lens, ISO, aperture, shutter, focal length, date taken).
• Download choices — tap Save and pick Original (full resolution) or Optimized (smaller, great for sharing).
• Faster galleries — album and Photos grids load and scroll noticeably smoother on large albums.
• Smarter People — face grouping is cleaner: fewer duplicate people, and each person now shows only photos they're actually in.
• Fixes — album delete is faster and fully cleans up, re-uploads no longer duplicate photos, and various sync/caching issues are resolved.

Please try: opening a few photos and checking the info sheet, saving both Original and Optimized, scrolling a large album, and tapping people in an album to confirm the right photos show.
