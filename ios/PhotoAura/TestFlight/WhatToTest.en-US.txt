What's new in 2.5

• Bookings — clients see their booking, payment schedule, Zelle details, stops with directions and a map, and can read and sign their agreement right in the app.
• Invoices — every invoice with what's paid and what's left, plus the invoice and signed agreement as PDFs.
• Gallery previews — galleries waiting on the final payment show watermarked previews with a banner; saving unlocks once it's paid.
• Studio tools — a Bookings tab to send agreements, mark payments received, add overtime or charges and mark galleries delivered.

Please try: signing an agreement, opening an invoice PDF, browsing a gallery preview, and on a studio account marking a payment received and adding overtime.
